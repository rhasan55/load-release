FIN-02: Resolve conflicting invoice revision
Observed: invoice-206 has $1,800 and $1,850 at revision 3. The projection excludes both and holds L-106.
Task: fetch the authoritative invoice from a sandbox or source export, confirm the external-to-load mapping with accounting, and record a newer source-backed version. Preserve both original events for audit.
Acceptance: exactly one current invoice amount reconciles in cents; both conflicting inputs remain traceable; replay creates no additional accounting record; the existing invoice never triggers a duplicate create.
Dependency: accounting owner selects the correct source record. Rollback: disable downstream writes, retain ingress and rebuild the read model from the durable event log.


What I would establish in the first sprint
  1. Agree on one fleet, one load lifecycle and the source of truth for every field.
  2. Map the existing team's backlog to acceptance evidence, dependencies and named owners.
  3. Run duplicate, delayed, conflicting and cross-fleet events in a sandbox; reconcile entity counts and cents.
  4. Review the exceptions with dispatch and accounting, then make an explicit rollout decision.
What this work establishes
A reproducible approach to integration debugging, evidence checks and delivery accountability. It includes an event projection, a separate SQL ledger and a release plan.
It does not establish live vendor compatibility, trucking regulatory compliance, production security, throughput or business impact. Revision numbers, POD verification and field ownership are assumptions for this sample.
