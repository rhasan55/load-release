# A focused first week

Start with one fleet and one path from delivery to billing. Use the existing team and systems.

| Work | Time | What Ion can review |
| --- | ---: | --- |
| Walk through the current process with dispatch and accounting | 3 hours | Where information comes from, who confirms it, and what stops billing. |
| Review the team's current work | 4 hours | A short list of priorities, with one person responsible for each. |
| Reproduce the billing and paperwork problems | 4 hours | Concrete examples the team can investigate and fix. |
| Check the fixes against the original records | 5 hours | Correct invoice and payment amounts, complete paperwork, and a list of anything unresolved. |
| Keep the team and owner informed | 4 hours | Progress, blockers, decisions needed and a short demonstration. |
| **Total project-manager time** | **20 hours** | **A clear decision on the next step.** |

The developers' work is estimated with them after reviewing the current software. This is a proposed starting point, not a promise to complete every integration in one week.

Before live use, dispatch and accounting review the results. If records do not agree, keep live changes paused and assign the remaining fixes.
